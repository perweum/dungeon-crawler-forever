## How the sources are fetched (`tools/fetch_sources.py`, 2026-11-02)
Licences above are unchanged. Downloads go to a cache outside git (default `~/dungeon-loop/sources/`, with `state.json`: upstream id, sha256 of the files used, last check time); nothing from the cache is committed or executed.
- **CMaNGOS classic-db** (https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db, GPL-3.0): `--check` compares `git ls-remote` of `master` with the stored commit; `--fetch cdb` makes a shallow sparse clone (`Full_DB/`, `ACID/`, `Updates/`) and builds one `classic-db.sql` = `Full_DB/*.sql.gz` with `creature_ai_scripts` / `creature_ai_summons` replaced by `ACID/acid_classic.sql` (as upstream's `InstallFullDB.sh` loads it). `ACID/` (CMaNGOS EventAI scripts) carries its own notice: GPL v2 "or (at your option) any later version", so GPL-3.0-compatible; we take spell IDs only. The `Updates/` SQL files (about 395 newer than the dump on 2026-11-02) are **not** applied: they need a MySQL server.
- **AtlasLoot Forever** (CurseForge project 1422985, author sliccer, GPL-2.0): **not downloaded**. No key-free route to the release exists (CurseForge's API needs a key, its CDN needs a key since 2026-07-16, and the Overwolf Platform Terms of Use, section 7.2(iv), forbid automated access to or monitoring of the CurseForge website). `--fetch atlas` copies `data.lua` + `droprate.lua` from the copy the maintainer's CurseForge app installed in the game's AddOns folder (read-only), and `--check` reports when their sha256 changed (the TOC `## Version` does not change with data updates).
